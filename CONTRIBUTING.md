# Contributing to Product Deconstructor

Contributions are welcome. The goal is to make Product Deconstructor more accurate, technically rigorous, useful, and reliable.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a branch for your changes.
3. Make your changes to `SKILL.md` or supporting documentation.
4. Test the skill against real products and realistic prompts.
5. Submit a pull request describing your changes and test results.

## Contribution Guidelines

### Skill improvements
- Keep instructions clear, actionable, and unambiguous.
- Preserve compatibility with short, natural user prompts.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and repetitive instructions.
- Do not introduce unsupported claims or fabricated product data.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts, inferences, unknowns, and proposals.

### Research quality
- Prefer primary sources and authoritative documentation.
- Verify quantitative claims against their original sources.
- Cite evidence close to the claims it supports.
- Identify limitations and conflicting evidence.

### Report quality
- Keep reports structured and decision-oriented.
- Use tables and diagrams where they improve understanding.
- Avoid unsupported metrics and generic recommendations.
- Check document formatting and references before submitting changes.

## Reporting Issues

When reporting a problem, include:
- The prompt you used.
- The expected behaviour.
- The actual behaviour.
- Relevant output or error messages.
- Suggestions for improvement, if available.

## Pull Requests

Keep pull requests focused. Explain:
- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- How you tested it.
- Any known limitations.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be released under the repository's MIT License.
